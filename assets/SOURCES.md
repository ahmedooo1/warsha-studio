## How the files were obtained (to check before sale)

The working environment used to build this version could not reach polyhaven.com or ambientcg.com directly. The original files, under their original Poly Haven / ambientCG names, were taken from public GitHub repositories that redistribute them (listed below). CC0 allows that redistribution, but the provenance of a copy cannot be guaranteed the way a download from the official site can.

**Before commercial release: download each asset again from its official page (links below), at the same size, and rebuild with `python3 tools/build_textures.py <folder> assets/tex`.** The names of the originals are the ones the script expects.

## Fallback

If a picture cannot be loaded (page opened as a local file, no network), the scene keeps the textures it draws itself, as before. Nothing waits and nothing breaks.
